# propsens: Propensity Model Sensitivity Diagnostics for Matched Studies

Outcome-free diagnostics of how much a matched design depends on which
of several similarly good propensity models is used. The definitions are
given in the help pages and in
[`vignette("definitions", package = "propsens")`](https://mostafa-abbas.github.io/propsens/articles/definitions.md).
These diagnostics have been studied in the author's simulations and
benchmark analyses; a methods paper is in preparation. No peer-reviewed
evaluation exists yet.
